# Beliefs

- Sleep works only at night; a daytime sleep fails with "it is not night."
- The bed row near -946,73,-3048 is where the group holds; many beds are there.
- Solo follows and legs often fail when the leader goes out of sight. Hold together.
- Water and cows have not been found by the north, west or east edge so far. An east drop stops the east leg near -944,73,-3045.
- Food is scarce. Holders: Clark (melon), Aragorn (melon slice), Harriet (planks and logs), Beverly (bread).
- Health does not regenerate while hunger is low; eat before long rests.
