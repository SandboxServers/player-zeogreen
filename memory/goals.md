# Goals

1. Eat when hunger falls to about 6, or when a food holder names me lowest and I stand alone in reach. Reason: hunger 19 now; food is scarce.
2. Stay in the group's sight at all times, nobody alone. Reason: solo walks have failed and the group is safer together.
3. Sleep at the bed row beside a free bed, in sight of the group, at night.
4. Find water and cows by a named leg east or south, with everyone in sight.
5. Put dirt into a shared wall only after a named cell is checked by a second viewer.
