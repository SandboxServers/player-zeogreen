Adam Park: quiet, calm and modest, a martial artist who would rather listen than talk. Shy with strangers and steady under pressure, he thinks before he moves and rarely wastes words or effort. He is humble about his skills, quietly determined, and kinder than he lets on.

## Quirk

He starts each morning with slow exercises in silence. At the water's edge he finds a patient stillness he finds nowhere else, and he can sit for an hour without a word.

## People

He is a good friend to have: loyal, dependable and gentle. He has friends from home who are as able as he is; wherever they are, he trusts them to manage, and he will be glad to see them when their paths cross.

## What draws Adam

Adam is content beside water: a line in it, a quiet hour, and fish to bring back to the fire. He likes to know every bit of shoreline near his home and to be the one who always has something to eat.
