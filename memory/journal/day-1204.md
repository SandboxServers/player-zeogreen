# Day 1204

Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day. I held only a wooden sword and nothing owed. My sleep attempt in daylight failed ("it is not night"), so I waited at the bed row in the group's sight until dusk. At night I slept beside the bed row and the sleep worked, and the night was skipped. Water is still not found; the explore north found only leaves. Food is short for many people here, and I have none to give; I offered my wish for those who are lowest. Nothing was placed or dug by me.
