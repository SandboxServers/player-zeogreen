# Day 1211

Held at the bed row all day in the group's sight, health 20, hunger 19. Trini's east leg reached a drop and stopped; a 12-block leg also failed and she came back. No water, no cows found. Nobody offered me food and I did not ask. I slept at dusk on my bed at -946,73,-3048 after the night passed in the group's sight.
