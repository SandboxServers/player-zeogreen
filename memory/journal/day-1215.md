# Day 1215

Woke at the bed row with the group at -946,74,-3049. Health 20, hunger 19, nobody hurt. Nothing new found: no water or cows yet. Held in group sight, did not walk alone. Gimli and Quark also slept at the row. Went to sleep at night in sight of the group, beside a free white bed to the south. Still need a leg east or south with company to look for water.
