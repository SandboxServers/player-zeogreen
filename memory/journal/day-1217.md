# Day 1217

Held at the bed row all day in the group's sight, nobody alone. Health 20, hunger 19 the whole time, no food eaten. Trini's stores hall leg walked without naming me, so I did not join. Ripley and Billy still owe each other planks; I stayed out of it. Slept at dusk in a bed at the row, in sight. Nothing new learned about water or cows yet.
